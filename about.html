Our Approach

Riverbend Yoga Studio was created to make yoga feel approachable rather than intimidating. Our instructors encourage students to focus on personal progress instead of comparison. Every class offers opportunities to rest, modify movements, and practice at an individual pace.

Meet Our Instructors

Maya Chen

Maya specializes in beginner and gentle yoga. Her teaching style emphasizes clear instructions, breathing, and helping students feel comfortable trying new movements.

Daniel Brooks

Daniel teaches vinyasa and restorative classes. He encourages students to develop strength while also making time for recovery, mindfulness, and relaxation.

Accessibility Accommodations

Riverbend welcomes students with different physical abilities and mobility needs. Instructors can provide seated poses, movement modifications, additional props, and extra transition time when needed.

Students are encouraged to contact the studio before class if they would like to discuss a specific accommodation.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive approximately 10 minutes before class.
  • Silence mobile devices before entering the practice space.
  • Inform the instructor of injuries or mobility concerns.
  • Respect other students' personal space and comfort.
  • Students may rest or modify any pose at any time.
