Classes for Every Experience Level

Riverbend Yoga Studio offers several class styles so students can choose a practice that matches their experience, energy level, and goals.

Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga focuses on comfortable stretching, balance, mobility, and breathing. It is ideal for beginners, older adults, or students who prefer a slower pace.

Vinyasa Flow

Vinyasa connects movement with breathing through flowing sequences. Instructors provide options for different ability levels so students can adjust the intensity of their practice.

Restorative Yoga

Restorative Yoga uses supported positions and longer holds to encourage relaxation and reduce physical tension. This class is a good option for students who want a calming practice.

Private Yoga Sessions

Private sessions provide one-on-one instruction based on individual goals. Sessions may focus on improving flexibility, developing a home practice, learning modifications, building confidence, or practicing specific movements.

Pricing Overview

Riverbend offers flexible options instead of requiring long-term commitments.

  • Multi-class pass: Discounted package of several classes
  • Monthly membership: Access to regularly scheduled classes
